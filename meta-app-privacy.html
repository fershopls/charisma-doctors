Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

“Insights” (the “App”) is a private, localhost-only dashboard used by its operator to view performance data for the operator’s own Meta advertising accounts. This policy explains the data the App handles.

Data the App accesses

  • Meta user ID and name, through the Meta login or access token used to connect the account.
  • Ad account metadata and insights: campaign, ad set and ad names; spend; impressions; clicks; CTR; CPC; CPM; reach.
  • A Meta Marketing API access token, stored locally on the operator’s computer.

How the data is used

  • Authenticate with Meta and retrieve the operator’s own advertising data.
  • Display that data in the local dashboard.

The App does not create advertising audiences, profile individuals, or make automated decisions about people.

Storage and sharing

All data is stored locally on the operator’s machine. The App has no remote database and does not transmit data to any third party other than Meta. The access token is kept server-side on localhost and is never exposed to the browser. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data.

Meta processes data under its own privacy policy when the App calls the Meta Marketing API.

Retention

Data remains on the operator’s machine until the operator disconnects the account or deletes the local token and cache files. The operator can delete them at any time.

Security

The App binds to 127.0.0.1 by default, so it is accessible only from the local machine. The operator is responsible for securing the device and the Meta access token.

Your rights

The App is used by its operator to access the operator’s own ad accounts. If you believe the App holds data about you, contact the operator on WhatsApp at +52 442 439 2916 to request access, correction, or deletion. You can also revoke the App’s access at any time in Meta Business Settings.

Children

The App is not directed to people under 18, and no data is knowingly collected from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use of the App means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? WhatsApp +52 442 439 2916.
